# Chapter 08 — ledger

| Folk claim (named retelling) | Record (document, page/section) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Wired says Maersk's synchronising controllers could, “in theory,” back up one another, but that the arrangement did not cover every controller being wiped simultaneously. | Gavin Ashton, “Maersk, me & NotPetya,” “Finding our feet”: disaster-recovery processes covered loss of a site or datacentre, but not loss of every controller at once; Maersk had lost them everywhere. Wired's caveated account agrees with the participant record, though “backup” is loose terminology for a current peer. | HOLDS |
| Wired says a blackout left one controller alive in Ghana. | Ashton, “Finding our feet,” confirms the directory restoration and first controller, but not the place or blackout. An official Maersk recovery report identifying the survivor and outage would settle it. | OPEN |
| BleepingComputer says M.E.Doc's update servers caused the initial infections. | Cisco, “Summary”: all installations its incident responders traced came through M.E.Doc's update system. | HOLDS |
| BleepingComputer says the updater lacked HTTPS and cryptographically signed binaries. | Cisco's incident-response report documents the compromised controls but does not state whether clients verified signatures. A period updater specification, binary or forensic report would settle it. | OPEN |
| BBC calls the event the “NotPetya ransomware attack.” | DOJ indictment ¶33, as an allegation: the ransomware purpose was a ruse, and files could not be recovered after payment. | WRONG |
| Darknet Diaries says Maersk had a functioning network after about nine days. | Maersk, *A View from the Other Side of a Crisis*, p.4 (“What we learned from the NotPetya Virus cyber-attack”): the network was rebuilt in “just 10 days” while cargo kept moving. The approximate timing is compatible. | HOLDS |
| Darknet Diaries says NotPetya ultimately cost Maersk USD 350 million. | Maersk, *Annual Report 2017*, printed pp.29 and 54: approximately USD 250–300 million. | WRONG |
