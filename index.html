Ten Ways In

Every famous breach has a folk version: one unpatched server, one password, the air-conditioning vendor. The folk version is never false and never the whole story. This book takes the ten categories of the OWASP Top 10:2025 and tells each through one real break-in and one real defense, from the indictment, the regulator's report, the vendor's own post-mortem, the commit. Each chapter ends in a ledger of what the folk story got wrong, and one thing you can now see in a system you run.
