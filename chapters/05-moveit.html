Chapter 5 · A05:2025 Injection

One Function, Three Patches

On 31 May 2023, Progress warned MOVEit Transfer customers about a critical vulnerability and published fixes for supported versions. The theft had already started: incident responders found the earliest evidence on 27 May, with data sometimes leaving within minutes of a web shell arriving. The familiar story is one zero-day SQL injection in a file-transfer product. That label is right. It hides the useful parts. The route into the query did not look like a form field, and the vulnerable function did try to escape its input. It escaped one use and missed another. The first patch changed that function; two more SQL-injection advisories followed within fifteen days. One function explains both the break and the defense.
