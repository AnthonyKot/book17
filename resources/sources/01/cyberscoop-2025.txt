Source: https://cyberscoop.com/court-reimposes-original-sentence-for-capital-one-hacker/
Fetched 2026-09-01 via WebFetch (summary; the article body was not saved). RETELLING (press),
used only for the post-remand status of the sentence. What the fetch reported:

- U.S. District Judge Robert Lasnik reimposed the sentence in November 2025: time served plus
  five years of supervised release with three years home confinement, 250 hours of community
  service, and the original $40.7 million restitution order maintained.
- Quoted from the judge: "The Court cannot find that a term of imprisonment would meet the
  need for any sentence to provide the defendant with needed medical care, or other
  correctional treatment in the most effective manner."
- The article says Lasnik acknowledged his "poor job of articulating the reasons" for the
  original sentence.
SUPERSEDED 2026-09-02: the judgment itself (Doc. 462, filed 2025-10-29) has since been fetched as
judgment-2025.txt. It differs from this summary: supervised release of 2 years (count 1) and 1 year
(counts 2, 4, 5, 8) with 3 years of probation credited, not "five years of supervised release"; the
residence restriction is a location-monitoring condition, not a term called "home confinement".
