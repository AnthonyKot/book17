How Are Attackers Found

Internal detection is when an organization independently discovers it has been compromised.

External notification is when an outside entity informs an organization it has been compromised.

Companies were notified of ransomware-related intrusions by an external entity 70% of the time.

Organizations were mainly notified by adversaries in 76% of investigations due to fully executed ransomware attacks detected by ransomware notes. External partners make up the remaining 24% of ransomware investigations.
