Excerpt: Member Commitment and Response; Technology Expertise

Since LinkedIn became aware on the morning of June 6 of the theft of approximately 6.5
million passwords, we have communicated to our members and the media regularly through
various channels, including the company blog, email, social media, and the LinkedIn
homepage.

The LinkedIn technology team has completed a long-planned transition from a password
database system that hashed passwords, i.e. provided one layer of encoding, to a system
that both hashes and salts the passwords, i.e. provides an extra layer of protection.

For security reasons, we cannot discuss certain details of our ongoing security upgrades.

We can confirm that all member passwords now are not only hashed, but also salted, to
provide an additional layer of security.
