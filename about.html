Method

The spine is the OWASP Top 10:2025, fetched on 1 September 2026. Every quotation from it is checked by machine against that fetch. Every claim about an incident carries a marker in the page source and a row in a claims file naming the document it rests on. The documents are the ones closest to the event: the criminal complaint, the consent order, the committee report, the vendor's post-incident review, the commit.

Each chapter's ledger sets the folk story against the record using five words: HOLDS, OVERSTATED, WRONG, MISSING, OPEN. People are named only as the record names them. Historical code already in the public record is quoted; nothing here is a recipe against a system still in service.

The checks prove that the quotes are real and the claims are sourced. They do not prove that the sources say what we say they say. That is checked by a review panel and, finally, by you.
